LungVision AI — pilot report

Research use only. Not validated or authorized for clinical diagnosis, screening, treatment decisions, or patient management. Pilot-scale results; not representative of a fully trained model.

Setup

Custom 3D residual U-Net (11.3 M parameters), trained from random initialisation on LIDC-IDRI (CC BY 3.0) at 1 mm isotropic, 64³ patches, Dice+BCE loss, Apple M5 Pro / MPS. Test set: 12 scans / 12 patients / 23 reference nodules from the patient-level held-out test split (split id 0d3c22b2a31122fd). This is a small subset of the 153-scan test split — wide uncertainty.

Known failure modes

  • Many false-positive candidates per scan (vessels, pleural/ chest-wall structures) — no false-positive-reduction stage yet.
  • Some scans have Dice 0 / missed nodules (see per_reference_nodule.csv).
  • Mean HD95 is dominated by distant false positives.
  • Single retrospective collection; no external validation. LUNA16 ⊂ LIDC-IDRI, so not independent.
  • Malignancy is not predicted: a confirmed-malignancy model is blocked (see reports/label_provenance.json).
